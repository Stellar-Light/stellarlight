# 2026-10-23: rerank hackathon prior-art candidates with a cross-encoder

## Scope

vetIdea and the builds search rank stored hackathon submissions by a blend
of word coverage and voyage-3 meaning scores. On the 27 working ideas of the
40 labeled ones, the hybrid matched list holds 95 of 99 same-idea builds but
only 45 reach the top 5; about 1 in 5 top-5 slots holds a build graded 0;
and meaning scores of 0.70 to 0.80 cover both wrong and right builds. The
gap is ordering. Does a cross-encoder reranker, which reads the idea and
each candidate together, order them better? An earlier cross-encoder test
on research documents was rejected; builds are a different task, so this
measures here.

## Method (fixed before any result was read)

- **Capture.** Lane K's capture (`../audits/2026-10-23-build-matching/`,
  1.9.91 at start and end): all 1,344 stored submissions, 140 labeled ideas,
  each idea's whole hybrid ranking with live meaning scores. Not committed
  (upstream descriptions).
- **Base.** This tree's `searchHackathonBuilds` replayed over the capture
  (as `replay.mts` does), hybrid, one row per submission. vetIdea reads the
  plain ranking (no package matching) and lists `priorArtTop` of its top 5;
  the builds route reads the ranking with package matching and shows its
  rows in order.
- **Reranker.** The embedding provider's `rerank-2` and `rerank-2-lite`. Query: the idea
  lowercased and trimmed, as both routes pass q. Document: the build's name
  and its one-line summary (`name. summary`), the fields every index row
  holds; the write-up `buildEmbeddingText` also reads is not in the capture
  or in the index rows. Candidates: the base ranking's top N = 50 (the
  union of vetIdea's and the route's top 50, scored in one call per idea and
  model; a cross-encoder scores each pair on its own).
- **Variants.** (a) reranker order, top 5; (b) reranker order with the
  evidence rule (`priorArtTop`) applied to the new top 5; (c) reranker order,
  top 5, keeping builds whose reranker score clears a floor (the floor
  replaces the evidence rule). For the builds route only (a) applies; it
  has no evidence rule.
- **Tuning, working ideas only (27).**
  - Model: the share of differently graded labeled pairs, within each
    idea's candidates, that the reranker orders correctly (ties count
    half), pooled over working ideas. Higher wins; a tie goes to
    rerank-2-lite. Only labels that exist before the run count, so the
    model is chosen before any new label is read. The other model then
    runs only on working.
  - Floor for (c): the highest value on a 0.01 grid that removes no build
    graded 2 from (a)'s top 5 on working, after the working entrants are
    labeled.
  - N stays 50 unless the working ideas show it clearly wrong (same-idea
    builds below rank 50 of the base).
- **Report**, per set (working, holdout, short, tests, fresh) and variant:
  builds graded 2, 1 and 0 and unlabeled builds in the top 5, and every
  swap by name and grade. Unlabeled builds that enter any top 5 get blind
  labels (shuffled sheet, rubric from `labels.json`, idea plus build name,
  event and summary cut to 600 characters) before final numbers.
- **Ship criteria**, per consumer (vetIdea against its listed five, the
  builds route against its top 5):
  - holdout: same-idea in the top 5 at least the base's, and wrong strictly
    fewer;
  - fresh: wrong in the top 5 fewer or equal;
  - short and test sets: same-idea in the top 5 not fewer.
  - If no variant passes, stop and report.
