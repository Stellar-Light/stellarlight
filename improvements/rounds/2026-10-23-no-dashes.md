# 2026-10-23: no em or en dashes in served text

## Scope

The owner reads em dashes (U+2014) and en dashes (U+2013) as a sign of
machine-written text and asked for them to be removed from anything the product
serves or shows: API messages, notes, hints, warnings, page copy, stored
blurbs, knowledge-note text. About 1,770 served-looking lines in about 140
files still had them. This ledger covers part A: `src/lib/repo-knowledge.ts`,
`src/app/(frontend)/**`, `src/components/**`, `src/lib/integrations/**`,
`src/lib/quality-artifacts.ts`, `src/blocks/**` and every route under
`src/app/api/**` except `src/app/api/hackathons/**`. Code comments are not
touched. Quoted upstream text is not touched.

Out of scope for part A (other lanes, and a part B to follow):
`src/lib/openapi-spec.ts`, `src/lib/changelog.ts`,
`src/lib/stellar-scout-skill.ts`, `src/app/api/hackathons/**`,
`src/lib/hackathon-*.ts`, `src/lib/vet-idea.ts`, `src/lib/event-in-query.ts`,
`src/lib/build-rerank.ts`, `src/lib/compact-view.ts`, `src/collections/**`,
`src/data/**`, `public/**`, `src/payload-types.ts`.

Target: 0 lines with a dash in part A, except lines listed as upstream text,
each with its reason. Measure: `improvements/audits/2026-10-23-no-dashes/`.
