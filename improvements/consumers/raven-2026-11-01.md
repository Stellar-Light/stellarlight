## What changed for you this window

- 2026-11-01 `` **[fixed]** The build sync reads an SCF page's siteUrls record as JSON, its object form first: a record it cannot read is could-not-check, never links-none, so a stored winner link is not dropped on a read it could not make. A DoraHacks link is dorahacks.io or a subdomain of it, never another host's /buidl/ path, and names are compared with their accents folded.
- 2026-11-01 `spec@1.9.116` **[fixed]** locateSource reads a stellar-cli command against the commands stellar-cli has: past the first run of words, reversed or plural. "Which stellar-cli file implements the keys generate command?" now ranks keys/generate.rs first, where it ranked 77th, and "Where does the stellar CLI extend a contract's TTL?" ranks contract/extend.rs first.
- 2026-10-31 `spec@1.9.115` **[fixed]** searchProjects' statusChecks no longer read a partial reading as a verdict: on rows found by meaning, whose dates arrive as Date objects, the DefiLlama checks said not yet checked; a contract with only one of its two counters read counted as quiet; an unparseable window and a negative TVL fell through to a hit; and $0 over a listing with a mapped slug DefiLlama no longer lists could hit. Each now reads its dates or answers could-not-check.
